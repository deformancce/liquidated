# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Crypto-market observers, traders, and audiovisual-data enthusiasts who want to perceive live order flow without continuously reading a conventional chart or tape.

## Product Purpose

Liquidated turns Hyperliquid public trade flow into one synchronized instrument: a live tape, a physical-modeling synth, and a liquid WebGL field. The landing page should make this unusual product immediately understandable and invite people into the live experience without presenting it as trading advice or a signal service.

## Brand Personality

Dark, physical, and precise. The experience should feel like a responsive instrument driven by real market forces: experimental enough to intrigue, controlled enough to trust, and never like a generic crypto dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid stock crypto imagery, decorative dashboard mockups, generic abstract gradients, repeated feature-card illustrations, neon-fintech clichés, and visual claims that are not produced by the real instrument.

## Design Principles

- Show the real instrument instead of illustrating an imagined product.
- Make the relationship between trade, sound, and motion tangible.
- Keep the landing page quieter than the live experience so the instrument remains the payoff.
- Treat market data as material, not as financial advice.
- Prefer one decisive visual moment over repeated decorative imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for core text and controls, preserve keyboard access, avoid color-only meaning, and provide reduced-motion behavior for non-essential animation.
